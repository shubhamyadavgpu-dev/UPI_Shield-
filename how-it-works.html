THE WORKFLOW

Five layers between deception and authorization.

WHY CONTEXT MATTERS

One signal can be weak. Several signals together can be dangerous.

UPI-Shield is designed around the idea that fraudulent payment behavior is often revealed by the combination of multiple contextual signals.

Layer 01

Signal Collection

Payment request and relevant contextual inputs enter the system.

Layer 02

Language Intelligence

Layer 03

Risk Engine

Layer 04

Human Intervention
