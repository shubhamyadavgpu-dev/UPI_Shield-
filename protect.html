VERIFY BEFORE TRUST

Don't verify the story. Verify the source.

Scammers often create convincing stories around electricity bills, banking issues, parcels, refunds and authority notices. UPI-Shield encourages users to independently verify the request before authorizing a payment.

SMART PAYMENT HABITS

Four habits that make digital payments safer.

01

Never rush because of a threat

Fake urgency is one of the strongest psychological tools used in social-engineering scams.

02

Verify outside the conversation

Use an organization's official website, application or known contact method instead of the sender's link or number.

03

Check the recipient carefully

Review the recipient identity and payment details before authorizing a transaction.

04

Treat unexpected payment requests carefully

Unexpected fees, refunds, penalties or verification payments deserve additional scrutiny.

05

Understand the warning

If a security system explains a suspicious pattern, use that information before deciding what to do.

06

When unsure, stop

A short pause is safer than authorizing a payment under pressure and trying to reverse it later.

A secure payment starts with a clear decision.
