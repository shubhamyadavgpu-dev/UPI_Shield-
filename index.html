Why UPI-Shield

Authentication answers "who?" Context answers "why?"

A legitimate user can unknowingly authorize a fraudulent payment. UPI-Shield adds contextual intelligence to the decision-making process.

01

Detect psychological pressure

Identify urgency, fear, threats and authority pressure that can manipulate users into making rushed payments.

02

Understand payment context

Connect the payment request with the surrounding message instead of treating the transaction as an isolated event.

03

Explain before intervening

Show users why a payment appears risky so they can make a more informed decision.
