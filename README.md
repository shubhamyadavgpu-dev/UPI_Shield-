# 📌 About The Project

**UPI Shield** is a FinTech security project designed to detect **digital payment scams, social engineering attacks, manipulation and coercive payment requests**.

Modern payment systems such as UPI provide strong authentication mechanisms, but authentication alone cannot determine whether a user was **psychologically manipulated into authorizing a fraudulent transaction**.

UPI Shield addresses this problem by analyzing the **context behind a payment request**.

Instead of asking only:

```text
"Did the user authorize this transaction?"
```

UPI Shield asks:

```text
"Why is the user making this transaction?"
```

The system analyzes contextual signals such as:

* 🚨 Urgency
* ⚠️ Threat language
* 💰 Payment pressure
* 🎭 Impersonation
* 🔗 Suspicious links
* 🧠 Social engineering patterns
* 📱 Scam-related communication
* 🔍 Suspicious payment intent

and provides a simple risk assessment to help users make safer decisions.

---

# 🚨 The Problem

Digital payment scams increasingly rely on **human manipulation instead of technical hacking**.

A scammer may convince a victim to willingly authorize a fraudulent transaction by creating fear, urgency or confusion.

### Common examples include:

| 🚨 Scam Type           | 📝 Example                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| ⚡ Electricity Scam     | "Pay immediately or your connection will be disconnected." |
| 🏦 Fake Bank Alert     | "Your account will be blocked unless you complete KYC."    |
| 📦 Fake Delivery       | "Pay ₹50 delivery charges to receive your package."        |
| 👮 Fake Police Warning | "Transfer the money or legal action will be taken."        |
| 🎁 Fake Prize          | "Congratulations! Pay a small fee to claim your prize."    |
| 💼 Fake Job            | "Pay registration charges to confirm your job."            |
| 💳 Fake Refund         | "Send ₹1 to verify your account for a refund."             |

The transaction itself may appear completely legitimate.

The **context is fraudulent**.

# 🧠 Threat Detection

UPI Shield looks for multiple contextual indicators.

## 🚨 Urgency Detection

Examples:

```text
"Pay immediately."

"Act now."

"Final warning."

"Your service will stop today."

"Complete payment within 10 minutes."
```

These phrases may indicate an attempt to pressure the victim into making a rushed decision.

---

## ⚡ Threat Detection

Examples:

```text
"Your electricity will be disconnected."

"Your bank account will be blocked."

"Police action will be taken."

"Your account will be suspended."
```

Threat-based communication is a common social-engineering technique.

---

## 💰 Payment Pressure

Examples:

```text
"Transfer ₹5,000 immediately."

"Pay the verification fee."

"Send the amount now."

"Complete the payment to avoid cancellation."
```

---

# 🧠 Why Context Matters

Traditional security systems often focus on:

```text
Authentication
       +
Authorization
       +
Transaction
```

UPI Shield introduces another layer:

```text
Authentication
       +
Authorization
       +
Transaction
       +
       ↓
    CONTEXT
       ↓
"Why is this payment happening?"
```

This is particularly important for **authorized fraud**, where the victim technically approves the payment but does so because of deception or coercion.
